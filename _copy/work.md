## Working together

Engagements begin with a conversation about what prompted the question. We agree on the scope, methods, intended outcomes and the form of the resulting work before beginning. Some inquiries are narrow and time-limited; others involve several related areas or a sequence of independently scoped engagements.

We work alongside the people who know the organization, involving them in the work and drawing on their experience. Our work is primarily investigative and advisory, and we don't assume operational responsibility for client systems or deliver production software. The conclusions are presented plainly, with enough evidence and reasoning to support the decisions that follow.
