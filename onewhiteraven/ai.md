## Writing/Editing

Articles on *one white raven* may be created with <abbr>AI</abbr> assistance that includes research, drafting, iteration, and citation. Where AI is used, it is assisting a human to accomplish a task. All assertions, citations, attributions, and content are checked and re-checked by that human to ensure accuracy, honesty, and readability.

All works are professionally copy-edited without the use of <abbr>AI</abbr>.

## Images

Every image is a photograph or a historical work, and every one is credited. We don't publish AI-generated images.

## Mistakes

Any errors are ours. If you find one, write to [hello@sixravens.io](mailto:hello@sixravens.io). Corrections are made on the piece itself, dated and visible.

## Why it matters

*one white raven* investigates how people create, interact with, and adapt to complex systems—and how those interactions shape what we believe, how we behave, and what ultimately happens. We draw on research across disciplines to examine these relationships, particularly where technology and artificial intelligence are changing their nature. transparancy about our own use of <abbr>AI</abbr> in that work provides the most complete, intellectually honest vantage point on those ideas.
